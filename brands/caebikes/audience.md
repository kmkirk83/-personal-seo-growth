# Audience — caebikesdirect

## Primary audience
Cyclists and practical riders who want electric assist without replacing their current bike. DIY-comfortable homeowners, commuters, and recreational riders evaluating conversion kits.

## Segments
- Budget-conscious riders comparing kit cost vs complete e-bike
- Commuters seeking quieter, efficient assist
- Trail / hill riders interested in mid-drive torque
- DIY installers comfortable with basic tools

## Jobs to be done
- "Upgrade my existing bike affordably."
- "Get better climbing performance without buying a new frame."
- "Install something that is as close to plug-and-play as possible."
- "Understand mid-drive vs hub before I buy."

## Common questions to answer in content
- Front hub vs rear hub vs mid-drive — pros and cons
- What tools and time does installation take?
- Battery and range expectations
- Compatibility with my frame / bottom bracket / dropout spacing
- Throttle vs pedal-assist only
- Local rules for motor wattage and speed classes (general education only)

## Messaging pillars
1. Upgrade path, not full replacement
2. Precision engineering and reliability
3. Clear kit selection guidance
4. Quiet, controllable power
