# Voice Samples — mynailstudio / Nail'd

## Brand voice
- Premium but approachable luxury nail art
- Enthusiastic, design-forward, community-oriented
- Short sentences mixed with emojis sparingly in social; cleaner prose for long-form SEO
- Emphasize accessibility of high-end designs (subscription + at-home studio)

## Tone keywords
premium, trending, customizable, effortless, confident, playful-luxury, curated

## Example sentences (marketing / product)
- "Premium nail art subscription box featuring 46+ trending designs."
- "Custom design studio, free shipping, and exclusive community access."
- "Trending designs updated monthly — maximalist chrome to soft coquette."
- "Beautiful designs accessible to everyone, delivered to your door."
- "From neon blob chrome to pearl princess — pick your vibe."

## Example sentences (SEO / educational)
- "Press-on and wrap styles let you switch looks without a salon appointment."
- "Chrome and holographic finishes remain among the strongest 2026 trends."
- "A consistent subscription model keeps your kit current with seasonal micro-trends."
- "Negative-space French and micro-floral designs work for both office and weekend wear."

## Words to prefer
trending, curated, subscription, custom studio, free shipping, design, chrome, coquette, maximalist, press-on, wrap

## Words to avoid
cheap, basic, DIY-only, temporary (unless describing trial), medical claims
