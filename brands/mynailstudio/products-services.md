# Products & Services — mynailstudio / Nail'd

## Core offer
Luxury nail art subscription service with customizable, high-end designs delivered to the door, plus a digital design studio.

Primary surface: https://mynailstudio.base44.app  
Related full-stack platform: piggy-nails-gallery (gallery, shop, subscriptions, studio booking, mobile)

## Subscription tiers (from marketing assets)
- Starter Box — ~$34.99/month — 3 nail wrap kits, mini files, care instructions
- Trendsetter Box — ~$99/month — 3 kits, exclusive design access, priority support
- VIP Box — ~$180/6 months — 4 kits/month, premium files, exclusive designs
- Elite Box — ~$360/year — 4 kits/month, aftercare kit, priority support
