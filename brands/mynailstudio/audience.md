# Audience — mynailstudio / Nail'd

## Primary audience
Women roughly 18–45 interested in beauty, fashion, DIY nail art, and subscription boxes.

## Segments
- Trend-conscious (TikTok / Instagram NailTok followers)
- Busy professionals who want salon-quality looks without appointments
- DIY enthusiasts who enjoy applying wraps/press-ons at home
- Gift buyers (subscriptions, limited editions)

## Platform priorities (from social strategy)
1. Instagram — visual community, product showcases, Reels
2. TikTok — growth, transitions, tutorials, viral audio
3. Pinterest — high-intent traffic, long-lived inspiration pins
4. Facebook — community, longer testimonials

## Jobs to be done
- "I want on-trend nails without booking a salon."
- "I want a predictable monthly nail kit that matches current aesthetics."
- "I want easy application and removal."
- "I want designs that photograph well for social."

## Common questions to answer in content
- How long do wraps last?
- Are they reusable?
- How do I apply / remove safely?
- What is the difference between tiers?
- Which designs work for work vs weekend?
- How often are new designs added?

## Messaging pillars
1. Trend access without salon friction
2. Quality and finish (chrome, pearl, 3D)
3. Customization via design studio
4. Subscription convenience and value
