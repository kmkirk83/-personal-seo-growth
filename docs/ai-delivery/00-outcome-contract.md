# Outcome Contract

**Product outcome**  
Personal zero-monthly-cost AI SEO/GEO automation system that generates, optimizes, and stages high-quality brand-aligned content for two owned properties.

**Target user**  
Sole operator of mynailstudio.base44.app and caebikesdirect.myshopify.com.

**Success metric (90 days)**  
≥ 20 published articles, measurable Search Console growth, at least one observed AI citation, operator time ≤ 2 h/week.

**In scope**  
Keyword research, brand-voice article generation, internal linking, draft publish to Shopify, staging for Base44, basic technical checklist, local log.

**Out of scope**  
Paid backlink networks, automated social commenting, multi-tenant SaaS, guaranteed rankings.

**Constraints**  
Zero recurring cost, local + free-tier LLMs only, human approval before live publish, minimal Shopify scopes.

**Acceptance criteria**  
1. Outcome Contract approved  
2. ADR approved  
3. First vertical slice produces one reviewed article per brand  
4. Shopify draft created successfully  
5. Operator can regenerate with ≤ 15 min review  
6. No secrets committed  
7. Explicit stop/rollback path

Repository owner (kmkirk83)
